# StatsRun Community

Welcome to the public community space for **StatsRun** — a data storytelling company helping teams turn data into clear, engaging stories.

This repository is used to share our public roadmap, collect product feedback, discuss ideas, and keep the community informed about what we are building next.

## What you can do here

### View the roadmap

Follow upcoming StatsRun features, improvements, and areas of exploration.

Roadmap items are directional and may change based on user feedback, technical priorities, and product strategy.

### Share feedback

Have thoughts on StatsRun, data stories, dashboard templates, AI features, or the overall product experience? We would love to hear from you.

You can share feedback by opening a GitHub Discussion or creating an Issue, depending on the type of input.

### Request features

If there is something you would like to see in StatsRun, open a feature request and describe:

- What you are trying to accomplish
- Why it matters
- Any examples or use cases that would help us understand the need

### Join discussions

Use GitHub Discussions for open-ended conversations, questions, suggestions, and community ideas.

## Roadmap status

Roadmap items may use labels such as:

- `now` — currently available or actively being worked on
- `next` — planned soon
- `later` — planned for a future release
- `exploring` — under consideration or research
- `shipped` — released
- `feedback wanted` — we are actively looking for input

## How to contribute feedback

Before opening a new Issue or Discussion, please check whether a similar topic already exists.

When sharing feedback, include as much context as possible:

- What were you trying to do?
- What worked well?
- What was confusing or missing?
- What would make the experience better?

Clear, specific feedback helps us prioritize the right improvements.

## Important note

This public roadmap is intended to provide visibility into our current direction. It is not a commitment to deliver specific features by a specific date.

Priorities, timelines, and feature details may change.
